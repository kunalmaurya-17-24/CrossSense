# Limitations and remaining experiments

The completed evidence is a reproducible **software pipeline on synthetic fixtures**, not a demonstrated satellite retrieval system with validated real-world accuracy.

1. **No real raster training/evaluation.** The official SEN12MS attempt stopped at the 50 MiB preflight cap (winter SAR archive alone 14.38 GB). No full or partial archive was transferred. The CDSE India manifest contains real metadata, including a SAR acquisition listed in original and COG forms; it does not contain downloaded image pixels. No credentials were supplied, and no paid service was used.
2. **Incomplete source provenance in standard SEN12MS files.** Author keys establish pairing, but exact acquisition times/cloud QA are unknown unless the user supplies extra information. The default strict time rule rejects them; an explicit author-pair opt-in records the unverified time/cloud conditions. MODIS labels are not automatically joined by the importer; land-cover is unknown unless supplied. SEN12MS optical L1C TOA and prepared CDSE optical L2A SR are different domains.
3. **Copernicus preparation boundary.** The code does not automatically calibrate or terrain-correct raw GRD SAFE products. Use SNAP or equivalent outside this package and record its steps/version. L2A scale/offset and cloud masks must be source-specific. A metadata flag cannot verify processing scientifically; original product/processing provenance must be retained.
4. **Small experiment.** Nine mock regions yield 20 training, eight validation and eight test patches. Test patches happen to contain one synthetic category in the observed split. Artificial shared patterns simplify correspondence; neither the metrics nor visual categories imply real geographic or semantic generalization. The four-epoch CNN did not beat the statistical baseline. Recall@10 equals one automatically with only eight gallery items.
5. **Limited uncertainty and relevance.** One training seed is measured. Query-bootstrap intervals condition on a fixed tiny gallery and correlated queries. Repeated seeds and independent geographic holdouts are needed. Exact-pair relevance can penalize an overlapping alternative acquisition; no exhaustive semantic relevance ground truth is available. F1@K is intentionally absent.
6. **Scale and projection.** Preparation reads scene arrays and considers SAR/optical scene combinations. Exact retrieval stores all vectors and evaluation computes full similarity matrices; large catalogues need windowed reads, spatial/temporal candidate indexes, chunked search and potentially FAISS. The split sweep can degrade toward quadratic work in dense areas. Use a suitable local metric projection; one UTM zone cannot cover global SEN12MS with reliable distances. Mixed-CRS manifests are rejected and must be reprojected to a common suitable regional grid.
7. **Model and sampling.** CNNs are trained from scratch with basic range clipping, no SAR despeckling, incidence-angle normalization, augmentation or pretrained remote-sensing backbone. Invalid pixels are zero-filled after masking, with no separate mask input. Batches from nearby geography may contain no usable negatives and produce zero loss; no spatially balanced sampler is implemented. Optical/SAR temporal and seasonal changes, speckle, layover and residual co-registration errors remain challenges.
8. **Compute and operational limits.** CPU execution and deterministic CPU resume were observed on Windows. CUDA was unavailable, so GPU execution, VRAM budgeting and cross-platform behavior remain unverified. Recovery is at completed-epoch boundaries. Checkpoints are local trusted pickle artifacts. Retrieval scores are uncalibrated cosine similarities. There is a CLI and research notebooks, no hosted application or production service.
9. **Data access limits are deliberate.** The downloader refuses unknown/oversized archives and logs exact outcomes; it does not bypass auth/TLS/registration or quietly fetch mirrors. Further manual real-data acquisition within reviewed terms is required. The optional Earth Engine route requires its own account, project and access terms and is not needed for the core.

Next steps: obtain a genuinely small paired SEN12MS subset from separated ROIs; recover times/QA or explicitly declare author-only pairing; prepare authenticated CDSE Pune-area pixels with calibrated SAR and offset-correct reflectance; extend India coverage to independent spatial holdouts; run several seeds and larger galleries; compare fixed baselines, random untrained encoders and trained models with region-aware batching; inspect genuine urban/rural/cloud/season/time-gap failures and report region-level uncertainty. Keep the original results even when a later model performs worse.

Accurate claims today: the package prepares and audits georeferenced paired fixtures; performs spatial holdouts and leakage checks; trains a normalized two-branch contrastive encoder; resumes CPU training reproducibly; retrieves and evaluates both directions against a statistical baseline; and produces inspectable logs, ranks, metrics and image examples. Real satellite accuracy, cross-region real-world generalization, semantic understanding and reconstruction are not established.
